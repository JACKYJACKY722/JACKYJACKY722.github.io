Dark Souls III

I must say, the story of Dark Souls 3 is unforgettable. Every NPC, boss, and even ordinary enemy in the game has a complete and compelling backstory. I would even say that the game's entire story could be made into a movie! And one movie might not even be enough to tell it all.

The boss battle music in Dark Souls 3 also left a deep impression on me. Each boss has its own unique boss battle music, which perfectly suits them. For bosses with multiple phases, the music becomes even more intense when switching phases.

Almost every boss fight in Dark Souls 3 left a deep impression on me—the backstory, the attack moves, the boss battle music, and the boss's design. Even though they were sometimes really hard to defeat,But at least I enjoyed the process of overcoming the difficulties.
